# Pocket Arcade

A small browser arcade with three games to play whenever you want.

## Games

- **Neon Snake** — Eat food, grow longer, and try not to crash.
- **Space Defender** — Shoot down enemies and protect your ship.
- **Brick Breaker** — Break the bricks and keep the ball in play.

## Play

Play Pocket Arcade here: https://nyvrixista.github.io/Pocket-Arcade/
